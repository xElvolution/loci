# loci and Qloo: the solution

**loci is the way home after hospital, built for both sides of care.** Clinicians write the plan and track it from their own workspace. Patients and families receive it with a code and follow it step by step at home. The loop is **plan, follow, track, adjust**, and Qloo's taste AI fills the days in between with comfort that fits the person.

Live: https://app.locicare.xyz (Try the demo: "See it as a clinician" or "See it as a patient", same demo patient on both sides, no sign-up)

## The loop

1. **Plan (clinician).** In the clinician workspace, start from one of twelve specialty templates, a stroke rehab or orthopedic physio structure template, or the team's own saved templates. Write each medicine schedule, care task, exercise and appointment, plus a plain "why" and a step-by-step "how", general instructions, warning signs and check-in thresholds. Preview the patient view.
2. **Send.** One click gives an 8-character code and a private link. The patient types the code in the app or opens the link.
3. **Follow (patient).** The plan appears as numbered steps in the clinician's words. Tick-offs, a two-minute daily check-in, an "ask my care team" thread, the when-to-call panel, the journey, the care circle and the Qloo comfort layer.
4. **Track (clinician).** Live adherence per item, check-in trends, flagged check-ins with "Mark reviewed", notes and questions, care circle status, an inbox for questions.
5. **Adjust (clinician).** Edit the plan in place. The patient sees "Your care team updated your plan" with exactly what changed.

## For the person and their family (free)

- **Redeem a code** from the care team, or start a journey yourself and copy the discharge letter.
- **The plan, step by step.** What to do, why your team asked, how to do it, and when, in the team's words.
- **Ask your care team.** A simple thread for practical questions; clearly not for emergencies.
- **Onboarding by specialty.** Twelve templates: maternity, surgery, orthopedics, cardiac, neuro rehab, pediatrics, mental health, oncology, respiratory, diabetes, elder care and general. Patients or caregivers can lead.
- **Today.** Medicines, care tasks and exercises at the times the team set, one tap to tick off. Next appointment and next milestone.
- **Daily check-in.** Mood, pain, sleep, appetite, energy and the plan's warning signs in two minutes. Streaks and badges reward showing up, never health values.
- **When to call.** If an answer matches a clinician's rule, loci repeats the clinician's words and shows call buttons for the team and the local emergency number.
- **The road home.** An animated road from hospital to home with milestones, appointments and two to four weeks of trends.
- **Care circle.** Invite by private link with roles. Shared errands that people claim and finish.
- **Comfort, by Qloo.** A soundtrack, shows, reading, meals near home within ticked filters (vegetarian, halal, delivery, soups, and more), gentle places nearby, and a good-day plan that weaves these around the plan steps.
- **Welcome-home card.** A shareable card with an arrival soundtrack, first meal and first night in. No health details.

## For clinicians and care teams (planned pricing)

- Own sign-up and workspace at `/clinic`, with colleagues in a care team.
- Patient list with status (draft, sent and waiting, following), flags and open questions, sorted by what needs you.
- Plan builder from specialty templates with why and how per item, general instructions, warning signs and thresholds; patient-view preview; send with a code.
- Per-patient tracking: today's tick-offs, 7-day adherence per item, five trends, flagged check-ins with review notes, notes and questions, circle status, live activity.
- Message thread per patient and an inbox of open questions. Update the plan after sending; the patient is told.

## How Qloo makes it personal

loci asks for a few favorites (an artist, a show, a book, a podcast, a game) and a home city. Qloo's Insights API returns:

- artists, shows, films, books and podcasts with high aggregate affinity to those favorites, with explainability so each pick says "Because of Norah Jones"
- restaurants near home that match all ticked tags, within a walkable or city radius
- cuisines the taste leans toward
- parks, gardens, museums, libraries and cafes nearby, optionally step-free or quiet

These become a day: wake up slow with one artist, lunch from a place that delivers, a quiet hour with a book, an evening with a show, wind down with another artist, with the medicine and exercise slots exactly where the team put them.

## Safety by design

- loci invents no medical content. Templates hold structure only.
- Flags are simple, visible rules written by clinicians.
- Qloo gets favorites, a city point and generic tags only. Never names, conditions or check-ins. Taste is never used to infer health or eligibility, and picks are labeled aggregate affinity.
- Consent switches, a consent log, export and one-step delete.

## Impact

- Fewer missed doses and exercises because the plan is visible at the right times to everyone helping.
- Earlier calls because warning signs are in front of people daily, in the team's own words.
- Less isolation and better mood because recovery days are built around what the person loves.
- Clinics get a low-cost, consent-based signal between visits for every specialty.
